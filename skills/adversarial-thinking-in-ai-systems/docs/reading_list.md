# Reading List

All techniques in this session are credited to their original authors.

## Foundations
- Szegedy et al., *Intriguing Properties of Neural Networks*, 2014.
- Goodfellow, Shlens & Szegedy, *Explaining and Harnessing Adversarial Examples*, ICLR 2015.
- Madry et al., *Towards Deep Learning Models Resistant to Adversarial Attacks*, ICLR 2018.
- Papernot et al., *Practical Black-Box Attacks against Machine Learning*, ASIACCS 2017.
- Geirhos et al., *Shortcut Learning in Deep Neural Networks*, Nature Machine Intelligence, 2020.

## Evaluation Done Right
- Athalye, Carlini & Wagner, *Obfuscated Gradients Give a False Sense of Security*, ICML 2018.
- Carlini et al., *On Evaluating Adversarial Robustness*, 2019.

## Robust Training & Optimisation
- Korpelevich, *The Extragradient Method for Finding Saddle Points and Other Problems*, 1976.
- Shafahi et al., *Adversarial Training for Free!*, NeurIPS 2019.
- Wong, Rice & Kolter, *Fast is Better than Free: Revisiting Adversarial Training*, ICLR 2020.

## LLM-Era Attacks & Practice
- Zou et al., *Universal and Transferable Adversarial Attacks on Aligned Language Models*, 2023 (arXiv:2307.15043).
- Andriushchenko, Croce & Flammarion, *Jailbreaking Leading Safety-Aligned LLMs with Simple Adaptive Attacks*, ICLR 2025 (arXiv:2404.02151).
- Greshake et al., *Not What You've Signed Up For: Compromising Real-World LLM-Integrated Applications with Indirect Prompt Injection*, 2023.

## African Evaluation & Sovereignty
- Adelani et al., *IrokoBench: A New Benchmark for African Languages in the Age of LLMs*, NAACL 2025 (arXiv:2406.03368).
- *AfroBench: How Good Are LLMs on African Languages?*, ACL Findings 2025 (arXiv:2311.07978).
- Jacaranda Health, *UlizaLlama* - open-access Swahili/Hausa/Yoruba/Xhosa/Zulu LLM for maternal health support. [huggingface.co/Jacaranda/UlizaLlama](https://huggingface.co/Jacaranda/UlizaLlama)
- Lelapa AI, *InkubaLM* - compact multilingual LLM for Swahili, Yoruba, isiXhosa, Hausa, isiZulu. [lelapa.ai](https://lelapa.ai)

See `docs/study_guide.md` for a plain-language explanation of each paper and why it matters for adversarial thinking in practice.
